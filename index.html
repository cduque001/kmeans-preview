If you want to understand machine learning, start with K-means.

Each dot marks a house's location. Choose three groups, so K is three.

K-means picks three starting centers. This dot is closest to purple, so it joins that group. Now every dot picks its nearest center.

Orange's center is off to one side. Average the dots' positions left to right, then up and down. Every dot counts equally. Those two averages give the new center: the mean. Do this for every group.

Now the centers have moved, so a dot might be closer to a different one. Check every dot again, update the groups, then move the centers again. Keep repeating until the groups stop changing.

That's K-means: assign, average, and repeat until the groups settle.
